Porpoise attributes its notification findings to PS5SX2 by Spyros
(GPL-3.0-or-later), including ps5/coreorbis/orbis-shims/ProsperoNotify.cpp,
and the notification JSON layout to LightningMods' payload SDK sample.
These upstream credits are preserved here.

Pixel Museum uses a bounded asynchronous native-only transport, with no in-app
trophy overlay or replacement sound. tools/notify-relay/museum_notify_relay.c
is the unchanged attributed Porpoise relay source; the embedded payload is
rebuilt locally with the public PS5 Payload SDK and libSceNotification stub.
No downloaded Porpoise release binary, proprietary sound sample,
RetroAchievements component, credentials, or remote badge download is included.
The system sound identifiers are passed to the console's local library.
