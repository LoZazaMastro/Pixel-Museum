This is a bounded editorial use of publisher promotional media. It is not labelled Creative Commons or blanket rights clearance. The five free-image records retain their actual licences; the depicted games and trademarks have separate rights.

## Explicit holds

- **Kondo:** the full E3 photo has verified source metadata and an adequate2304×3072original, but Commons returned429 before its download. Further requests stopped. Small cropped portraits are not substituted.
- **Shibuya:** official interview pages are identified, but candidate asset responses did not decode as images. The Square Enix West policy explicitly excludes the Japanese Final Fantasy franchise and cannot serve as its permission basis.
- **Addis:** archival access to the Sumerian Game printouts/slides is not reproduction permission. No image is invented or copied from an entire monograph to fill the slot.
- **Umurangi:** five genuine publisher `path_full` URLs contain `1920x1080`, but the bytes decode at963×542,913×514,1053×592,1053×592 and944×531. The larger library hero endpoint returns404. They remain excluded rather than enlarged.
- **Korb/Barrett:** the documented Commons Korb portrait is648×864 and does not depict both performers. It is excluded.

The JSON's `holds` are research records, not runtime placeholders. Source access and image dimensions are never presented as a licence grant. Standalone features without an admitted cover remain media-pending; no automatic parent-cover substitution is performed.
