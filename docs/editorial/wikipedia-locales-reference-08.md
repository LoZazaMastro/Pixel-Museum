## Translation decisions

- **Disgaea:** The two-year sleep, succession goal, merchandise, numbered sequel, anime and ports remain distinct. Official game titles and platform names stay unchanged. Phantom Brave remains a spiritual sequel, while Disgaea D2 retains the original cast and additional characters. The exact English calls Disgaea 1 Complete an HD remake; this inherited wording is translated, not silently changed to remaster. Terminology adjudication would require a separate source correction.

- **Oddworld:** Stranger remains a bounty hunter, moolah remains the named game currency, and living ammunition retains its different tactical uses. The Oddboxx collection statement stays in the paragraph about its December2010 release. The 11February2022 date applies to Xbox One and PlayStation4; later PS5 playability is explicitly subsequent, without inventing a PS5 release date.

- **Shantae:** The 2002 predecessor remains distinct from the 2010 sequel. Risky Boots stays a pirate. The added mode keeps its name Magic Mode, the Android release remains Japan-only, and every Director's Cut platform/date stays in the same paragraph. Favorable reviews and direct story continuation remain separate from the port list.

- **Sekiro:** Wolf, Ashina, Sengoku, Miyazaki and Tenchu remain the same subjects. The setting is fictionalised Japan rather than a claim that its events are historical. The new-IP intention is distinct from Tenchu's impetus. Mixed opinions concern difficulty despite broader praise; sales remain more than ten million as of September2023, not a current sales verification. An English extra space and awkward grammar are rendered naturally only in translation.
