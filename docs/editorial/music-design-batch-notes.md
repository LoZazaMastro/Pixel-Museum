## Limits that must survive integration

The Riven tape was not streamed or transcribed in this pass. Its catalogue record establishes an object and date, not every speaker or spoken line. The VGHF donor's permission to that institution is not a blanket third-party streaming licence. Its overlap note must survive any later footage selection.

ACMI's Umurangi record states that the game is not in its collection. The separately identified photographic print is in its collection. An institutional page does not turn a developer screenshot or a personal photograph into an openly licensed museum asset.

Shibuya's interview is a participant recollection. Ishii's interview has a later portal date but identifies its original publication as 2020. A programme listing proves the programme's identity, not the contents of a video that was not transcribed. The cards do not attribute all early Final Fantasy art to one artist.

The Rez feature discusses cross-sensory design intent without diagnosing artists, creators or players. Its 2016 Area X images must not be labelled as original 2001 captures. Bastion and Journey album dates remain distinct from game dates; individual recording and performance credits are retained.

Celeste's selected-code licence is not a licence to distribute the commercial game's art, levels or audio. Freely offered Bastion arrangements likewise do not establish unrestricted reuse of the sheets or soundtrack.

## Illustration work still to do

Every card has a concrete image or audiovisual brief, but this batch supplies **no historical media file and no unreviewed image URL as a ready asset**. The first useful acquisitions are a correctly dated Riven object image, an authorised Kondo portrait, a credited Journey performer image, the exact Faulkner photograph, Ustwo's identified team photograph and official version-labelled game captures. Source archives or studios must be credited at item level. Official publication is evidence of provenance, not proof of an open licence.

Original diagrams can explain timing, layering, input windows and production handovers without pretending to be historical documents. They should use the museum's restrained paper/ink palette with a single accent, remain clearly labelled interpretation, and never recreate a copyrighted score or fabricate an archival photograph.

The next acceptance pass should check the forty historical claim/source pairs, approve the merge or connected-feature decision, obtain and caption each image, inspect actual card layouts and only then consider localisation and a PS5 content build. No new interactive study is required to make this writing useful.
