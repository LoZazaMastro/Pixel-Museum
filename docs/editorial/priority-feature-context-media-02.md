## Verification and licensing

All admitted originals were downloaded from the photographers' public Flickr publications, fully decoded, compared visually with the relevant subject, and hashed. Every original has at least 1,600 native pixels on its long edge. The licences are explicit CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC or CC BY-NC-SA 2.0, retained individually with creator and licence links. The files are not retouched. Rights in depicted hardware, brands and game artwork remain distinct from the photographers' licences.

The public Flickr search was filtered for stated reuse licences, followed by inspection of the actual photo pages and original-size records. Search labels alone are not identity proof. One instructive rejection is `267917009`: the seemingly convincing Wii-shaped object is actually a **television remote**, with Japanese channel and volume labels and a source description identifying it as such. It is not admitted as a Wii game controller. The selected actual controller is the different photograph `1348345547`.

Other rejections remain documented: the first DK Bongos photo's actual original is only 1,280 x 960; an old cabinet URL now exposes related-photo data rather than its requested photograph; one otherwise promising photo is truncated and fails decoding. The latter is not forced through a permissive image decoder. A high-resolution scan of EA's poster is all rights reserved without a separate reuse basis and remains outside the admitted payload.
