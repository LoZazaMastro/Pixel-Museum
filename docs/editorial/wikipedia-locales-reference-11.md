## Reading and translation decisions

- NFL Blitz: The sport is American football. Arcade exaggeration remains distinct from realistic simulations. The2005 fictional league and later EA licensed return are separate. Late hits mean blows after the play has ended; their removal remains linked to NFL input. The2022 Arcade1Up changes remain separate from the earlier relaunch. English grammatical number errors are rendered naturally without altering the source.

- Mattel Auto Race: The inherited first all-solid-state handheld claim remains attributed, not newly established. Mechanical controls and power switch remain explicit exceptions. Calculator hardware, nine-volt battery, red LEDs, beeps, Klose hardware and Lesser additions/512-byte code are distinct facts. Three lanes, gears1through4 and increasing speed remain unchanged. MissileAttack, BattlestarGalactica, SkiSlalom and the Soviet clone keep their names and dates.

- Populous: Considered the first god game retains the qualification. Sales are more than four million; levels are more than500. The deity seeks to defeat opposing followers and increase its own population. PeterMolyneux design and Bullfrog Lego-board prototype remain distinct roles. Year-end accolades are nominations, not invented wins. The two sequel titles remain unchanged.

- Radiant Silvergun: Six shot types and the sword remain separate tools. Ten developers, outsourced Gonzo animation, ST-V architecture and professional high-score testers retain their roles. The first release is May1998, with the Saturn version two months later. Genre revival is attributed to journalists rather than presented as a new editorial verdict. Ikaruga is a spiritual sequel;2011/2022/2023 worldwide ports retain exact platform associations.
