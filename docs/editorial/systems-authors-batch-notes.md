# Systems and authors: editorial batch 06

Eight complete English features, 32 cards, pending independent source and placement review. They are additive proposals, not eight admitted destinations. No master, locale, original Playhub, runtime, package or console was changed. All media briefs remain unselected.

The canonical text exists once in each `body` array. Four `story_headings` correspond to four card records by `body_index`. Each card contains a specific source locator and a separate media brief. Overlapping existing articles were read in full; their section counts and exact body hashes are recorded in `editorial_audit`.

Each card contains 80–85 whitespace words. Each publication charge includes the complete feature title and summary plus every relevant heading and paragraph. The larger of whitespace and Unicode lexical counts is used, with a maximum of 195 words; actual charges range from 188 to 195. Different pages, mirrors and translations of one publication count together. No text is quoted verbatim. Sources retain their rights and citation does not grant rights to images or footage.
