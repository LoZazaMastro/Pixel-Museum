# Local album inventory proposal (not implemented)

Measured against build/runtime-catalog.json on2026-10-21, after cue projection:11,018,784bytes,1389entries,530rooms,1067tracks. The projected soundtrack room payload totals425,698bytes; track records total340,822bytes (mean319.42, maximum439bytes). Largest current room is8480bytes. At the same real schema,100,000cues alone imply31,942,080bytes, excluding narrative, images and room metadata. The global16MiB native catalogue limit must remain.

Suggested schema: each room index retains title, composer, invitation, source/credit summary, track_count, and a package-relative `tracks_asset_file` plus contentSHA256 and declared byte size. Its local JSON sidecar contains version1 plus the complete projected tracks array, including exact verified start/end seconds and individual creator/source fields. No deduplication of titles, dates or attribution strings is needed to change identities. Keep full authoring audit data separate as today.

The music card invitation and timeline icons consume only the room index. A bounded single worker reads/checks/parses an album when its card gains focus; adjacent music-card inventories may prefetch disk-only. Opening the room can show its normal loading animation until ready. Never parse a large album or hash its file on the frame/audio thread. Runtime sidecars stay in the app package, without HTTP.

Suggested limits for an initial fixture:4MiB encoded per album,2048cues,16rooms per exhibit (current parser limits), at most active+one adjacent decoded inventory. Check path prefix, traversal, declared size, actual length and SHA256 before JSON parse. Immutable shared_ptr inventories stay alive for active playback while LRU eviction drops only unused rooms. Generation cancellation discards old results; closing playback must not await worker IO. Malformed/missing/corrupt sidecars produce a bounded room error; legacy inline tracks continue to work.

Tests before implementation acceptance: a1000+cue album must open after ordinary global catalogue parse;1280index records remain under16MiB; full titles/composers/source fields round-trip;2048limit+malformed boundaries; corrupt/truncated/hash/source-version rejection; rapid focus/cancel/reopen and active-room ownership; resident inventories bounded; no IO on draw/audio; package manifest includes every referenced sidecar. Actual PS5 load/seek latency remains a separate measurement.
