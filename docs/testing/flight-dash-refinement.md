# Flight and Dash refinement

Flight now has twelve authored openings and a finite result. An opening crosses the camera at z=0.28, where its projected width exceeds the whole viewport, rather than disappearing while still a small central rectangle. A geometric pointer identifies the next opening and whether the current flight line fits through it. The world uses the available game area without the old boxed view and side score panel. Initial/repeated gyro calibration pauses forward movement; manual stick steering remains usable throughout. Passed and missed gates have separate geometric progress marks and tactile events. A perfect route receives a check; an imperfect completed route receives a retry symbol.

The actual study completes twelve openings with stick input and with synthetic angular samples at 30, 60 and 120 Hz. These tests exercise the real gyro filter and physics, but do not establish physical PS5 axis orientation, latency or framerate. Calibration/moving-pose rejection, manual override, focus/disconnect/reconnect, finite finish/replay, missed gates,12000 stress frames and the seven-study Arcade suite pass. Three real4K EGL renders have no GL errors.

Dash retains its three tested platform rooms and original mechanics. Its focus handling previously stopped new commands while update kept consuming dash duration and gravity. Focus loss and disconnection now freeze the entire update. A dotted direction arrow and a consistent diamond for available dash/refill make the action clearer; charge and landing visuals animate, world text labels are removed, and control glyphs remain at the bottom. All three rooms complete without falls at 30, 60 and 120 Hz using both stick andDpad. Midair refill, landing, no-spam, buffered jump/checkpoints, finite input and explicit update-while-focus-lost checks pass, as do the other movement and original audio regressions.

Evidence: `build/research/flight-dash-refined/verification.json` and its logs/renders. The native build includes Portal, Flight, Dash and the latest Credits correction. No FTP transfer was performed.
